About All Timez

Trusted by apparel buyers for four decades.

All Timez, also known as All Timez Kids Pants Wholesaler, is a Dadar West, Mumbai-based kidswear and fashion apparel wholesaler focused on kids pants that move quickly on retail shelves. Since 1981, the store has supported local retailers, resellers and bulk buyers with dependable everyday fashion, practical fits and market-ready styles.

From regular school-friendly bottoms to cargo, jogger, straight and baggy fits, All Timez helps buyers source practical kidswear with a wholesale-first mindset. The team understands colour demand, comfort expectations and repeat-purchase basics for children’s apparel stores.
